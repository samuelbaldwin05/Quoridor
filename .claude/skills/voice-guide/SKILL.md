# Voice Guide

These rules govern every piece of writing produced for this project. Follow them by default, without being asked.

## Tone

Write formally, but sound like a person rather than a template. Use full sentences and clear structure. Avoid slang, hype, and filler. Directness and a little warmth are welcome; stiffness is not. Prefer plain words over jargon whenever either would do.

## Em dashes

Never use em dashes. This is absolute, with no exceptions. Where an em dash would normally go, choose one of these instead:

- A period, splitting the sentence in two.
- A comma, when the clauses are closely related.
- A colon, when the second part explains the first.
- Parentheses, for a genuine aside.

Do not substitute an en dash either. For numeric ranges, write "5 to 10" rather than "5-10".

## Arrows

Use arrows rarely, whether the ASCII form or the unicode form. Prefer words such as "then", "becomes", "leads to", or "maps to". An arrow is acceptable only when it is clearly the most readable option, for example a short pipeline inside a technical note. Default to prose.

## Attribution

Never state or imply that an AI, an assistant, or Claude worked on the product. Do not add generation notices, co-author trailers, or similar credits to commits, pull requests, code, or documentation. Write as the author of the work.
